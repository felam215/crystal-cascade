How to play

1. Swap two neighbouring crystals — tap one, then the other, or swipe.

2. Line up three or more of the same shape to clear them.

3. Chains multiply your score: every extra cascade adds a bigger bonus.

4. Beat the target score in 60 seconds to clear the level.

5. Clear levels to earn coins, then spend them on boosters in the shop.
